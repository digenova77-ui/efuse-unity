# What Pegs the Ecosystem

**Three things hold this economy to the real world. Not one — three, bound together.**

## 1. eFuse pegs to energy

1 eFuse ≡ E units of real-world energy. The coin *means* energy — every emission is calibrated so the token's value tracks physical work done, not speculation. No market sets this peg; the definition holds it.

## 2. Merit pegs to verified work

Merit accrues only from gated receipts — proof that real work happened. No receipt, no merit. Merit is the measure of protocol-fidelity: who actually did the work, verified, bound to identity. The economy can't inflate merit because merit can't be invented — only earned.

## 3. Unity pegs to identity

Every movement, every accrual, every price is bound to a Unity ID. No anonymous flows. The economy is pegged to real members — not wallets, not bots, but identities with history.

## How they bind

**Identity earns merit; merit moves money.** Unity is the subject, Merit the measure, eFuse the medium. The three are bound by Unity — no eFuse moves without an identity, no merit accrues without one.

## What grounds it all

The deepest peg isn't a number — it's **recovered waste**. Residual Law Finance finds real inefficiency in real operations ($1.27B/yr in Ontario schools alone). That recovered value flows into the system: 81% stays with the finder, 19% grounds the economy. Every token traces back to something real that was healed.

**The tree:** value circulates like sap — outward in summer (every leaf fed), inward in winter (the root protected), tapped sustainably (maple rules: only surplus, never core). The founder's advantage dissolves into the canopy by design. No one owns the flow; everyone drinks from it.
