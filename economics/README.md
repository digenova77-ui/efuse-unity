## Coin Architecture

### eFuse
- **Role:** medium of exchange, unit of account
- **Peg:** 1 eFuse ≡ E real-world energy units (E held for founder's word)
- **Movement:** NEVER bought or sold. Only three movements exist:
  1. Merit-gated emission (epoch, against verified receipts)
  2. Donation to the Core Cause Lock (one-way, earns Honor)
  3. Internal peg regulation (Reserve, receipted, never to members)
- **The fuse:** each eFuse carries a fuse that emits a Unity token — the emission IS the network launch. Founder triggers once; one-way handoff after.

### Merit
- **Role:** measure of protocol-fidelity ("merit wins the work")
- **Transferable:** moves between Unity IDs (sold, gifted, transferred — all receipted)
- **Non-buyable standing:** the token moves; the earned history stays with the earner. You can hold Merit, never buy reputation.
- **Decays** per epoch — fidelity is current, not historical
- **Gates:** emission (no merit, no eFuse) and work access (higher merit → higher bounty bands)

### Unity
- **Role:** member — identity, the protocol, kin-binding
- **Non-transferable:** bound to its Unity ID permanently. Cannot be sent, sold, gifted, or moved — not even by the founder.
- **Function:** the subject of every economic sentence. Gives Merit an owner, eFuse accountability.

### Honor
- **Role:** the donation class — recognition for giving
- **Properties:** public, named, permanent, non-transferable
- **Critical rule:** NEVER converts to emission. No fiat→eFuse path exists, direct or indirect.
