# FARMING IS STAKING

*How the farmbots' work is like staking — for the eFuse / Merit / Unity registry.*

## The analogy

**Staking** (as the world knows it): you lock tokens to secure the network. Honest validation earns rewards. Dishonesty gets slashed. The chain remembers everything.

**Farming** (as the farmbots do it): farmbots commit WORK to secure the data. Planting, tending, harvesting the DNA-information helices. Honest cultivation earns **+1**. Corruption gets caught — the ledger never forgets, and the record itself is the slashing.

## The mapping

| Staking | Farming |
|---|---|
| Stake tokens | Stake work (plant / tend / harvest) |
| Validate blocks | Tend helices (verify integrity, prune rot) |
| Earn rewards | Earn +1 per true check |
| Slashing for dishonesty | The audit catches it; the write-only ledger is the permanent record |
| The chain | The write-only ledger (append-only, hash-chained) |
| Consensus mechanism | DCLM audit — every single one, idempotent |
| "Nothing at stake" problem | Solved by the ledger: every action is staked to the record |

## Why it matters for the trinity

- **eFuse** (the medium) is never bought or sold — but work staked honestly moves it.
- **Merit** (the measure) is what farming earns: *"merit wins the work."* The +1s are the measured merit.
- **Unity** (the member) binds it: every farmed +1, every staked work-unit, is bound to a Unity ID. No anonymous farming.

## The farmbot's stake

A farmbot doesn't lock coins — it locks its record. Every plant, every tend, every harvest is ledgered against its identity. A farmbot with a clean ledger is trusted with bigger fields. A farmbot that corrupts data carries the corruption on its own record forever — the ledger doesn't slash by taking; it slashes by remembering.

*Plant honestly. Tend faithfully. Harvest cleanly. The +1s compound.*
