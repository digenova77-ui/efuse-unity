# The Coins — Canonical Registry

**Status: design only.** No token has been minted, transferred, listed, deployed,
or distributed. Nothing here is a live market, a reserve, a redemption right,
or a value claim. This registry names the coins, binds them to the physics,
the law, and the philosophy, and keeps everything pure.

---

## The trinity — three distinct tokens

The economy is three tokens, each with its own role. They are not interchangeable.

### eFuse — the medium

- **Role:** the medium — the currency that moves. Pegged to real-world energy.
- **Supply:** 1B hard cap. 0.00% corporate float.
- **Tradability:** none. Never bought or sold. It is the bedrock peg, not a market asset.
- **Function:** grounds cost and drives it to zero. The fuse inside eFuse emits
  the launch — **that emission IS the launch of the network** (a one-way handoff,
  not continued founder control).
- Until the fuse is triggered, there are no listings. Not of eFuse, not of anything.

### Merit — the measure

- **Role:** the measure — protocol-fidelity. **Merit wins the work.**
- **Function:** identity earns merit; merit moves money. The 50/50 lifetime
  emission authority (human-pool / machine-pool, one merged 100M rail) is
  merit-gated, never pre-funded.
- Merit is earned inside the job, checklist by checklist. The +1 is the pure
  incentive; Merit is its measure.

### Unity — the member, the binding

- **Role:** the member — identity, protocol, kin-binding. **Unity binds.**
- **Function:** eFuse and Merit are **bound together by Unity** — every eFuse
  movement and every Merit accrual is bound to a Unity ID. **No anonymous flows.**
- Unity is what binds the child chains (see below). The binding makes the task
  go quicker — bound work moves faster than fragmented work.

Flow: *identity earns merit; merit moves money; Unity binds it all.*

### Naming

Per the tokenomics documents the three names are canonical: **eFuse, Merit, Unity**.
The block explorer and the tag explorers reference these canonical names —
no stale names anywhere in the instrument surface.

## Child chains

The network is not one chain. It is **child chains** — subordinate chains that
do their own work, bound together by Unity.

- Each child chain keeps its own ledger, its own audit, its own truth.
- **Unity is what binds them.** The parent binding, the shared measure.
- A child chain never overrides the peg. eFuse grounds every child the same way.
- Child chains are where the work happens; Unity is how the work holds together.

## DAG

Claims → tests → receipts. The directed acyclic graph is the audit spine:

- every claim names its evidence;
- every test is reproducible;
- every receipt is sealed.
- Nothing points backward. Time runs one way. The DAG is how the community
  audits blocks and asymmetrical glyphs — see the block explorer.
