Canon §VIII — zero 2D card UI; free world (looking costs nothing); data yes, cards no; every interface is a facet, every facet a valid view; refraction is the truth-display mechanism.

## 1. DESIGN PRINCIPLE — one geometry, three readable layers

Like the historical stone carried one decree in three scripts so each script deciphers the others, this artifact carries the economy's logic in three readable layers. **All three layers say the same thing** — the peg, the trinity, the flows, the tree — each in a different 3D language. A viewer who reads any one layer can cross-check it against the other two. That cross-checking IS the self-deciphering.

| Layer | 3D language | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) VISUAL FORM** | Trinity as interlocking diamond-geometric forms | The peg (bedrock), Unity (bind-ring), Merit (measure-helix) — what each token IS |
| **(b) SPATIAL FLOW** | Channels carved through the stone | Summer-outward / winter-inward / tap-outward-by-rule circulation — how value MOVES |
| **(c) STRUCTURAL RELATIONSHIPS** | The tree as the stone's growth structure; the peg as the foundation it stands on | Roots/trunk/canopy, the starch store, the fuse scar, the 19% infeed and 81% bypass — how everything RELATES |

**Label-material language** (how claim-status reads in form — applied everywhere):
- **[LAW]** → deepest relief cut (≥ 4 cm into the surface). The root of everything cuts deepest.
- **[DERIVED]** → shallow etched relief (1–2 cm). Present, lighter.
- **[HELD]** → **uncarved matrix: visibly rough, unfinished stone.** Honest absence. What is HELD is NEVER faked with decoration — the cut awaits David's word. This is the Iris rule made physical.
- **[LAW-DECLARED]** (ruling not yet sealed in canon/code) → cut to full depth but with a visibly open joint: finished form, unfinished seam — decided in word, awaiting the final cut.

## 4. STRATUM 1 — PEG BEDROCK (0.0–1.2 m): the foundation the stone stands on

**Economic element:** the eFuse energy peg — what grounds the ecosystem. **Layer: (a) form + (c) foundation.**

- **Geometry:** the lowest 1.2 m of the monolith, the widest band (full 3.6 × 2.4 m footprint, hexagonal section). Darkest, densest material in the stone (see §7). It does not move, has no channels passing through it except the two below — **it is the bedrock the whole economy stands on.**
- **The peg register:** on the approach face, a carved band 0.5 m tall running the full 3.6 m width. In 3D letterform (extruded glyphs, 4 cm relief — [LAW] depth): the peg definition expressed as geometry, not words — a sequence of carved symbols: ONE DISC ≡ ENERGY-WAVE GLYPH (the "1 eFuse ≡ E" relation as two interlocked forms). Beside it, **the E facet: a single hexagonal facet 0.8 m across left as UNCARVED MATRIX** — rough stone, visibly awaiting the cut. [HELD] — the mechanism is described fully; the digit is his.
- **The emission vault:** inside the bedrock, a sealed chamber of EXACTLY bounded volume — two balanced halves (50M human / 50M machine — design-decided ED-DECIDED-20261004-5050-V1, marked on the vault door in shallow etch [DERIVED]). The chamber is sealed: no inlet, no outlet except the single calibration conduit rising into the trunk (the eFuse_i = merit_i / E path — one tube, §5.3). **Finite volume = capped supply (100M lifetime). The vault can never be enlarged.**
- **Material:** near-black faceted crystal, highest polish in the stone — the peg is the hardest truth and reads as such. IOR reference 2.42 (architectural reference for the builder's transmission material, not a physics claim).

## 5. STRATUM 2 — ROOT ZONE (1.2–2.4 m): winter, the starch store, the infeed

**Economic elements:** winter inward flow; the Peg Regulation Reserve (starch store); the onboarder 19% infeed; the 81% bypass. **Layers: (b) flow + (c) relationship.**

- **Geometry:** the monolith's base flares into **five root buttresses** — tapered hexagonal prisms angling down-out at ~35° from vertical, each 1.6 m long, merging into the floor lattice (§2). Roots = infrastructure + the creator's capacity to keep building.
- **The starch-store chamber:** a hollow ovoid chamber at the center of the root zone, ~1.0 m tall × 0.8 m wide, reachable ONLY by the winter channels (§5.4). Its walls are the thickest in the stone (25 cm). A single inspection slit (4 cm wide, full chamber height) on the approach face shows fill level as physical depth — **winter storage is visible, receipted, and bounded: the chamber has a fixed volume; it cannot be overfilled (protection, never accumulation).**
- **Winter channels:** from the crown, **nine smooth grooves** (3 cm wide, 2 cm deep) converge down the trunk's facets and spiral into the chamber's top. They narrow continuously as they descend — **the 0.0→1.0 tilt is a smooth narrowing, never a gate or a switch: no phase cliffs.** Each groove's walls carry shallow etch marks at intervals — but the calibration notches are UNCARVED (rough) — **[HELD]: winter trigger thresholds await David.**
- **The 19% infeed conduit:** a single channel 12 cm bore entering the stone from the floor edge on the approach-left, at 1.6 m height — **real recovered value entering the system from the real world.** It runs 1.2 m into the stone then TRIFURCATES into three 7 cm branches:
  - one descending into the peg bedrock (PEG_SUPPORT — grounding the energy peg);
  - one rising to a sealed side-vault fused to the trunk's flank (CORE_CAUSE_LOCK — the Eden-locking vault; its address plate is a BLANK UNCARVED PANEL — [HELD]: the lock address is UNKNOWN, no placeholder rendered as live);
  - one exiting the stone's approach-right face as an open spillway (PLAYGROUND_FUEL — surplus funding the mesh bounties).
  
  **The trifurcation node is UNCARVED MATRIX** — the three branches are cut, but the flow-split geometry (bore diameters at the split) is left rough, visibly awaiting the cut. **[HELD]: the three-way split ratio is his.**
- **The 81% bypass:** a separate broad groove 20 cm wide carved into the floor itself (not the stone) that passes within 0.5 m of the monolith's base and continues past it — **touching nothing, entering nothing.** ACKNOWLEDGED_OFF_SYSTEM made physical: the first thing the approaching viewer reads is that most recovered value passes the system by and the system claims none of it. Carved in the floor lattice, [LAW] depth.
- **Honor tablets:** fused to the trunk's left flank at 1.4–2.2 m height, a vertical stack of **up to twelve thin stone tablets** (30 × 20 × 3 cm each), each slightly offset — **append-only: tablets are added at the top, never removed, never altered, never spent.** The donation inflow: a hairline channel (1 cm) from the infeed conduit to the Core Cause vault, passing behind the tablets. (Honor = the permanent record of donations.)

## 6. STRATUM 3 — TRUNK SPINE (2.4–4.8 m): the trinity interlock and the circulation

**Economic elements:** Unity/Merit/eFuse interlock; summer outward flow; merit accrual; DCLM rights/writes spine. **Layers: (a) form + (b) flow.**

### 6.1 The trinity interlock — layer (a)

At the trunk's waist (3.4–4.0 m), the three tokens exist as three physically interlocked diamond-geometric forms — they cannot be separated without breaking the stone:

- **eFuse — the medium / bedrock disc:** a flattened octahedron (disc-like, 1.2 m diameter × 0.35 m thick) embedded horizontally through the trunk's core at 3.4 m — the same dark material as the peg bedrock, continuous with it via the calibration conduit. It does not rotate, has no clasp, no handle, no market face — **it cannot be picked up, traded, or moved: unsold bedrock.** ([LAW-DECLARED]: never bought or sold.)
- **Unity — the member / bind-ring:** a torus (major radius 0.9 m, tube radius 0.14 m) encircling the trunk at 3.7 m, passing THROUGH the merit helix and the eFuse disc's edge — physically interlinked like chain links. The ring is continuous except for **one visibly OPEN JOINT** — a 6 cm gap with finished, polished ends facing each other across the gap. **[LAW-DECLARED]: Unity is immobile — the joint is open because binding is by identity, not by transfer; the bound-sale is killed.** The ring carries the Unity-ID register: small identity-notches (one per bound identity class: person / corporation / silicon agent) as 4 cm relief glyphs around its outer face — [LAW] depth.
- **Merit — the measure / accrual helix:** a spiral staircase of **24 step-blocks** winding up the trunk's surface from 2.4 m to 4.8 m (each step 25 cm tread, 10 cm rise, protruding 18 cm from the trunk face). Each step is an individual block — **merit accrues block by block, only from gated receipts: no receipt, no block.** The steps are fused to the trunk (they cannot be detached or moved between identities — [LAW]: merit was accrual-only per Unity ID; per the ruling the ECONOMIC VALUE may transfer but the steps themselves never leave the earner's trunk — see below). Older turns show a slight weathering gradient (recessed 3 mm per turn) — **fidelity is current: the decay principle** (digit HELD).
- **The merit transfer joint:** at the helix's base, one step-block is cut as a SEPARABLE CAP — a block that can lift 5 cm on hidden geometry but remains cabled to the trunk by a short stone tether (15 cm). **[LAW-DECLARED, open joint]: transferred Merit = economic value moves; the tether = earned standing/history stays with the earner and cannot be bought.** The tether is the purity distinction made physical.

### 6.2 Summer channels — layer (b)

- **Twelve grooves** (3 cm wide, 2 cm deep) rise from the trunk's core at 2.6 m, **branching as they ascend** (each groove bifurcates twice: 12 → 24 → 48 terminals), exiting at the crown's branch tips (§5.3). **Summer: sap flows up and out; every leaf fed.** The grooves widen slightly toward the terminals (gradient-driven, equalizing — flow spreads where it exits). Carved [LAW] depth at the trunk, shallowing to etch at the finest branchlets.
- The grooves pass AROUND the eFuse disc (never through it — the peg is not a conduit for summer flow) and INSIDE the Unity ring (all flow is identity-bound).

### 6.3 The calibration conduit — layer (b)

- A single 5 cm tube running inside the trunk from the emission vault (§4) up through the eFuse disc to the merit helix base — **the eFuse_i = merit_i / E path.** Its upper end terminates at an UNCARVED collar — **[HELD]: without E the conduit is incomplete; the pipeline refuses emission.** The builder models the collar as rough matrix visibly interrupting the tube.

## 7. STRATUM 4 — CANOPY CROWN (4.8–7.2 m): the tree's crown, the fuse scar, the taps

**Economic elements:** the canopy (every leaf fed); the fuse (founder advantage dissolved); tapping (maple rules). **Layers: (b) flow + (c) relationship.**

- **Geometry:** above 4.8 m the monolith's hexagonal section SPLITS into **seven branches** — tapered octahedral prisms angling outward at 20–35° from vertical, lengths 1.6–2.4 m. Each branch bifurcates once. **Forty-eight branch tips** each terminate in a small octahedral "leaf" facet (12 cm) — the summer channel terminals (§6.2) exit through the leaf facets. Every leaf fed.
- **The fuse scar:** the central eighth branch — the trunk's own apex — does NOT branch. It terminates at 6.4 m in a **spent-fuse scar: a cleanly severed hexagonal face, polished, with a single one-way barb** (a 10 cm cone pointing outward-down, fused to the severed face). **ARMED → TRIGGERED → SPENT, one way; the founder's advantage dissolved into the canopy by construction.** The scar faces the sky — the handoff is complete and visible. [LAW-DECLARED] depth with the barb in [LAW] cut.
- **Tap apertures (the maple rules):** on the trunk's right flank at 4.4–4.8 m, **three surplus pockets** — smooth bulges (30 cm diameter) where summer channels visibly pool. From each pocket, ONE tap aperture: a 4 cm bore narrowing through an **iris constriction** (a carved diaphragm ring reducing the bore to 2 cm at its throat) before exiting the stone. **The four maple rules in form:**
  1. *Only in season* — apertures draw ONLY from the surplus pockets, never from the trunk core, never from the starch chamber: no channel connects the taps to the root zone.
  2. *Limited taps* — the iris throat physically limits flow; the bore cannot be widened without breaking stone.
  3. *Only mature trees* — the aperture mouths are capped by **uncarved stone plugs** — [HELD]: the maturity threshold (pools healthy + peg holds + reserve funded) is undecided, so the taps are visibly sealed until it is.
  4. *The tree heals* — each aperture's surround carries a carved growth-ring band (concentric etched rings): every outflow receipted, season over season.
  
  **Tapping is the system releasing surplus outward through rules — the apertures point AWAY from the founder's side of the stone; founder extraction has no geometry here at all.**

- **Register II — THE REGISTER OF FLOWS (layer b):** channel-plan diagrams — the summer grooves as rising forked lines, winter grooves as converging lines to a chamber dot, tap apertures as constricted exits, the 19% trifurcation as one line becoming three (with the split node left rough — HELD). - **Register III — THE REGISTER OF GROWTH (layer c):** tree-grammar glyphs — root strokes gripping a ground line, a trunk stroke conducting upward, branching strokes to leaf dots, the fuse scar as a terminated apex stroke with the one-way barb. Reads: *how everything relates.*

**Self-deciphering rule:** the three registers are aligned vertically — Register I's disc glyph sits directly above Register II's calibration conduit line and Register III's ground line. A viewer reads down the column: form → flow → growth, three scripts, one decree. The column alignment is the deciphering key, and it is spatial, not textual.

## 10. MATERIAL & LIGHT — for the three.js builder

- **Primitives:** monolith body — custom extruded hexagonal sections (ExtrudeGeometry) per stratum, or a single LatheGeometry with hexagonal radial segments; branches — stretched OctahedronGeometry; bind-ring — TorusGeometry; helix steps — BoxGeometry blocks on a helical path; channels — recessed TubeGeometry grooves (model as dark inset tubes slightly below the surface, or actual CSG subtraction if the pipeline supports it; inset tubes are acceptable); glyphs — ExtrudeGeometry from 2D glyph paths, depth ≥ 2 cm; root buttresses — tapered CylinderGeometry (6-sided).
- **Materials (MeshPhysicalMaterial):** 
  - *Peg bedrock & eFuse disc:* near-black, transmission 0.35, roughness 0.15, ior 2.42 (architectural reference), clearcoat 1 — the hardest truth, highest polish.
  - *Trunk & branches:* smoked teal-grey crystal, transmission 0.55, roughness 0.3.
  - *Uncarved matrix (all H-surfaces):* matte rough stone, transmission 0, roughness 0.95, slightly warm grey — unmistakable against cut crystal.
  - *Law-depth cuts:* inner faces polished bright (roughness 0.1) so the deepest cuts catch light.
- **Light — the truth light:** ONE PointLight inside the trunk core at 3.6 m (the "primordial efusion" point), warm-white, modest intensity, distance-limited to the stone. All other scene light is neutral ambient. **Refraction is the truth-display mechanism** (Canon §XVII): what the viewer sees is the internal light refracted through the faceted geometry — never a texture pasted on, never emissive text. (The light is architectural analogy — "TRUTH is the light" — not a physics claim.)
- **Floor:** the ground plane within 8 m of the stone carries the diamond-lattice pattern (procedural line geometry or a subtle normal-mapped shader — builder's choice, must read as crystal lattice, not tile). The 81% bypass groove is cut into it (§5).
- **Performance note:** the stone is ONE merged static mesh group (plus the point light). No per-frame logic. Looking is free.
